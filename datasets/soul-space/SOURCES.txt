11) Primary scripture quote selection — Soul Space (2026-10-21)

- datasets/soul-space/bhagavad-gita/entries.json (17 records)

Selection scope: non-narrative verse excerpts aligned with resilience, patience, hope, self-regulation, compassion, forgiveness, truthfulness, humility, and ethical action. No narrative/story summaries were added in this scripture expansion.

Qur'an: the 12 new Arabic verse lines are copied from the bundled Tanzil Quran Simple Clean source; retain the full ayah text and Tanzil attribution/license. English and Bengali fields are Soul Space-authored renderings, not QuranEnc published translations. Existing Rowwad and Saheeh translation JSON snapshots must remain unchanged and follow QuranEnc's seven conditions.

Bhagavad Gita: 15 Sanskrit excerpts were added with Soul Space-authored English and Bengali renderings. The bundled K.T. Dhammapada: 20 Pāli verse records were selected from datasets/soul-space/dhammapada/pali.txt (ETCBC/dhammapada, MIT). English and Bengali fields are Soul Space renderings, not published translations. Verify verse numbers and exact punctuation against upstream before production release.

This selection is a starter expansion, not completion of the requested per-sector target. Public-domain status of historic editions in the USA does not establish copyright clearance in Bangladesh.
