8) License review and source eligibility
File: datasets/soul-space/LICENSE_REVIEW.txt
This register records which sources meet the current Soul Space use case, which have only been approved as candidates, and which remain excluded pending verification. Do not treat an approved candidate as integrated until its full source text is committed.
